# AI Seller Enablement — User Guide

Version: 1.0
Author: Vanessa Miranda

## Purpose

This demo simulates an AI-powered seller enablement platform that helps frontline sales managers identify performance gaps, prioritize coaching, and trigger proactive interventions.

The data is synthetic and created solely for demonstration purposes.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1 — Open Dashboard
The landing page displays regional seller performance and overall enablement health.

### Step 2 — Select a Seller
Choose any seller from the sidebar to view:

- Revenue attainment
- Pipeline conversion
- Training completion
- Skill profile
- AI risk score

### Step 3 — Review AI Recommendation

The AI evaluates seller signals and recommends actions such as:

- 1:1 coaching
- Product certification
- Discovery skills workshop
- Manager escalation

### Step 4 — Trigger Alert

If predefined thresholds are breached, the platform automatically flags the seller for intervention.

Example triggers:

- Revenue attainment below 70%
- Training completion below 80%
- Conversion decline greater than 15%
- High-risk skill gap detected

---

## Manager Workflow

1. Review seller KPI
2. Validate AI recommendation
3. Assign coaching action
4. Schedule follow-up
5. Monitor improvement over time
