# AI Seller Enablement — Solution Architecture

Version: 1.0

## Business Objective

Enable sales leaders to move from reactive coaching to proactive, AI-assisted seller development.

## Data Flow

CRM / LMS / Product Usage
          │
          ▼
  Seller Performance Layer
          │
          ▼
 AI Rules & Risk Scoring Engine
          │
          ▼
 Recommendation Engine
          │
          ▼
 Manager Dashboard & Alerts

## AI Components

### Inputs

- Revenue attainment
- Pipeline health
- Training completion
- Certification status
- Skill assessment

### Processing

- Rule-based prioritisation
- Weighted risk scoring
- Intervention recommendation

### Outputs

- Coaching priority
- Recommended learning
- Escalation alerts
- Manager action list

## Governance Principles

- Human-in-the-loop approval
- Explainable recommendations
- No automated employment decisions
- Audit trail for interventions
